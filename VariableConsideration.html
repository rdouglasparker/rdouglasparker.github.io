Exercise 2 · Most Likely Amount Method

Ridgeline Elevator Care

Since 1988, Ridgeline Elevator Care has provided maintenance services for elevators. On January 1, 2027, Ridgeline obtains a contract to maintain the elevators in a 50-story office tower in Charlotte, NC for 10 months and receives a fixed payment of $93,000. The contract specifies that Ridgeline will receive an additional $46,500 at the end of the 10 months if there is no unexpected delay, stoppage, or accident during the year. Ridgeline estimates variable consideration to be the most likely amount it will receive.

1.Assume that, because the building sees a constant flux of people throughout the day, Ridgeline is allowed to access the elevators and related mechanical equipment only between 3 a.m. and 5 a.m. on any given day, which is insufficient to perform some of the more time-consuming repair work. As a result, Ridgeline believes that unexpected delays are likely and that it will not earn the bonus. Prepare the journal entry Ridgeline would record on January 1.
