The facts

Balsam Gap Painting Company is a commercial and residential painting contractor in Haywood County. The company closes its books on December 31, 2026. Its controller has asked you to (1) sort the year-end trial balance items that might be reported as cash, and (2) prepare the December bank reconciliation, which the bookkeeper skipped while the company was busy with fall projects.
