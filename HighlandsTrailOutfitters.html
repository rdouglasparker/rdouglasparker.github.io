The facts

Highlands Trail Outfitters' fiscal year ends December 31. The following transactions involving receivables occurred during 2024:

  • March 17, 2024 — Accounts receivable of $2,600 were written off as uncollectible. The company uses the allowance method.
  • March 30, 2024 — Loaned an officer of the company $33,000 and received a note requiring principal and interest at 7% to be paid on March 30, 2025.
  • May 30, 2024 — Discounted the $33,000 note at a local bank. The bank's discount rate is 8%. The note was discounted without recourse and the sale criteria are met.
  • June 30, 2024 — Sold merchandise to Saluda Mercantile for $21,000. Terms of the sale are 4/10, n/30. Highlands Trail Outfitters uses the gross method to account for cash discounts.
  • July 8, 2024 — Saluda Mercantile paid its account in full.
  • August 31, 2024 — Sold stock in a nonpublic company with a book value of $5,900 and accepted a $6,900 noninterest-bearing note with a discount rate of 8%. The payment is due on February 28, 2025. The stock has no ready market value.
  • December 31, 2024 — Highlands Trail Outfitters estimates that the allowance for uncollectible accounts should have a balance equal to 4% of the gross accounts receivable balance of $680,000. The allowance had a balance of $21,000 at the start of 2024.
