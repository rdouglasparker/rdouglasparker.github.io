The facts

On January 1, Year 1, Nantahala Instruments, Inc. issued 10% bonds with a face amount of $50 million, dated January 1. The bonds mature in Year 21 (20 years). The market yield for bonds of similar risk and maturity is 12%. Interest is paid semiannually.

A different market yield
