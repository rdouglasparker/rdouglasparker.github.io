Extra Credit Case: The Quarter-End Request

Apply the conceptual framework and ethical reasoning to a covenant-driven accounting request and a standard-setting controversy.

Instructions

This optional case is open to every student and is worth up to 2 extra-credit points.

  1. Enter your WCU student ID and select Load my case. Your dollar amounts and answer order are generated from your ID.
  2. Work all three parts. Use a minus sign for a loss.
  3. Select Check my work as often as you like. Your extra credit is your percent correct × 2 points.
  4. When finished, select Print / Save as PDF and upload the PDF to the Canvas extra-credit assignment.
