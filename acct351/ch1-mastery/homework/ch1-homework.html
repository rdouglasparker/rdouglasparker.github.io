Homework: Environment & Conceptual Framework

Cash vs. accrual, assumptions and principles, elements, qualitative characteristics, and measurement.

Instructions

  1. Enter your WCU student ID and select Load my problems. Your company names, amounts, and situations are generated from your ID, so every student's packet is different — but yours is the same every time you load it.
  2. Work all five problems. Use a minus sign for negative amounts. You may enter amounts with or without dollar signs and commas.
  3. Select Check my work as often as you like. Each answer is marked ✓ Correct or ✗ Incorrect, and each problem shows how many you have correct.
  4. When finished, select Print / Save as PDF and upload the PDF to the Canvas assignment.
