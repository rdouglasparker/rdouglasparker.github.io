Extra Credit Case: The Loan Application

Adjust a start-up's first-year income statement before it goes to the bank.

Instructions

This optional case is open to every student and is worth up to 2 extra-credit points.

  1. Enter your WCU student ID and select Load my case. Your amounts and answer order are generated from your ID.
  2. Work all three parts. Round to whole dollars.
  3. Select Check my work as often as you like. Your extra credit is your percent correct × 2 points.
  4. When finished, select Print / Save as PDF and upload the PDF to the Canvas extra-credit assignment.
