Step 3 — What will the trial balance catch?

Omitting an entry entirely leaves debits and credits equal. The trial balance can't detect it.

Takeaways
  • Cash received in advance → liability. Paying a payable → not an expense. Dividends → not an expense.
  • A balanced trial balance only proves debits = credits.
