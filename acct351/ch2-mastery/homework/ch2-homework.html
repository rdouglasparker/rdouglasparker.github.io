Homework: The Accounting Process

Journal entries, adjusting entries and their effects, statements and closing, and cash-to-accrual conversion.

Instructions

  1. Enter your WCU student ID and select Load my problems. Your company, amounts, and transactions are generated from your ID.
  2. Work all five problems. Account lists are in alphabetical order. Round to whole dollars, and use a minus sign for negative amounts.
  3. Select Check my work as often as you like. Each answer is marked ✓ Correct or ✗ Incorrect.
  4. When finished, select Print / Save as PDF and upload the PDF to the Canvas assignment.
