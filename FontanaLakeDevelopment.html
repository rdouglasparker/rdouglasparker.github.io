The facts

The fiscal year ends December 31 for Fontana Lake Development. To provide funding for a new project, FLD issued 5% bonds with a face amount of $500,000 on November 1, Year 1. The bonds sold for $442,215, a price to yield a market rate of 6%. The bonds mature October 31, Year 21 (20 years). Interest is paid semiannually on May 1 and November 1 and is determined using the effective interest method.
