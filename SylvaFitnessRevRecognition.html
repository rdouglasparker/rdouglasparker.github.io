Part 1

New membership with yoga discount voucher

Sylva Strength & Fitness (S&F) is a health club in Sylva that offers members various gym services. S&F offers a deal whereby enrolling in a new membership for $700 provides a year of unlimited access to facilities and also entitles the member to receive a voucher redeemable for 25% off yoga classes for one year. The yoga classes are offered to gym members as well as to the general public.

A new membership normally sells for $720, and a one-year enrollment in yoga classes sells for an additional $500. S&F estimates that approximately 40% of the vouchers will be redeemed. S&F offers a 10% discount on all one-year enrollments in classes as part of its normal promotion strategy.
