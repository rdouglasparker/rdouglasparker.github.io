The one question to ask

    Assets − liabilities = shareholders’ equity

Rules that never bend

  1. No gains or losses on transactions in a company’s own shares.
  2. Retained earnings can be debited in a buyback, never credited.
  3. Par value only splits the credit; record what was received.
  4. No liability for a dividend until it is declared.
  5. Small stock dividend: fair value. Split as a dividend: par. True split: no entry.
Paid-in capital—share repurchase
Created when shares are retired below their original price or treasury shares are resold above cost. Never below zero.
Treasury stock
Issued but not outstanding; recorded at cost and subtracted from equity.
Dividends in arrears
Skipped cumulative preferred dividends; disclosed, not recorded.
AOCI
The running total of other comprehensive income.
