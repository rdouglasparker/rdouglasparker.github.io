Rules that never bend

  1. Estimates change prospectively. Never restate.
  2. Depreciation method changes are changes in estimate: prospective.
  3. Prior years’ effects go to retained earnings, never current income.
  4. LIFO to FIFO: the tax goes to income tax payable. A lower-income change gives a deferred tax asset.
  5. Inventory errors offset after two years. No entry once both years are closed.
Retrospective
Recast prior statements as if the new method had always been used.
Prospective
Apply the change to the current and future periods only.
Prior period adjustment
The after-tax effect of an error correction, added to beginning retained earnings.
Impracticable
The information needed to recast the past is not available.
