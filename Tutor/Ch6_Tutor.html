The big picture

  1. 1. Identify the contract with the customer
  2. 2. Identify the performance obligations: distinct promises, including material rights
  3. 3. Determine the transaction price, including variable consideration
  4. 4. Allocate the price by relative standalone selling prices
  5. 5. Recognize revenue as each obligation is satisfied, at a point in time or over time

Quick formulas

  1. Allocation: price × (standalone price ÷ total standalone prices).
  2. Material right: (extra discount) × price × redemption rate.
  3. Expected value: Σ outcome × probability. Most likely amount: the single likeliest outcome.
  4. Percentage of completion: costs to date ÷ total estimated costs; revenue to date = price × percentage.
  5. Loss on the whole contract: recognize all of it in the first year it is expected, under either method.
Performance obligation
A promise to transfer a distinct good or service.
Material right
An option the customer would not get without the contract, such as an extra discount.
Breakage
Prepaid rights the customer is expected never to use.
CIP and billings
Construction in progress holds costs plus profit recognized; billings on construction contract is its contra account.
