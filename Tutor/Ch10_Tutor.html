The one question to ask

  • What did we give up?
    Cash, a note, shares, or another asset, at fair value
  • ↓
  • Add every cost necessary to get the asset ready for its intended use
  • ↓
  • Costs of using it afterwards (training, insurance, taxes) are expensed

Rules that never bend

  1. Capitalize all costs to acquire and prepare the asset.
  2. Land is never depreciated; improvements are.
  3. Lump sum: allocate by relative fair values.
  4. Noninterest note: record the present value, not the face.
  5. Exchange: fair value, unless no commercial substance (no gain).
  6. R&D is expensed; patent legal costs are capitalized.
Commercial substance
Future cash flows change because of the exchange.
Avoidable interest
Interest that could have been avoided without the construction. Capitalize the lower of this and actual interest.
Asset retirement obligation
A legal duty to retire an asset; recorded at present value and added to the asset’s cost.
Goodwill
Price paid minus fair value of net identifiable assets.
