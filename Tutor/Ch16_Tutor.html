The four-step method

  1. Income tax payable: taxable income × current-year rate.
  2. Ending deferred tax: cumulative temporary difference × enacted rate for the reversal year(s).
  3. Change in deferred tax: ending − beginning.
  4. Income tax expense: payable + increase in DTL (or − increase in DTA).
Deferred tax liability (DTL)
Future taxable amounts: tax owed later on income already in the books.
Deferred tax asset (DTA)
Future deductible amounts: tax saved later on expenses already in the books.
Permanent difference
Never reverses, so no deferred tax. It changes the effective tax rate.
More likely than not
A likelihood of more than 50%.
