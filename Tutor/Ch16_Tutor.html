Key idea for this problem

    The four-step method

    1. Income tax payable: taxable income × current-year tax rate.
    2. Ending deferred tax balance: cumulative temporary difference × enacted rate for the reversal year(s).
    3. Change in deferred tax: ending balance − beginning balance.
    4. Income tax expense: the plug. Payable + increase in DTL (or − increase in DTA).
    Deferred tax liability (DTL)
    Future taxable amounts. Taxes owed later on income already reported in the books.
    Deferred tax asset (DTA)
    Future deductible amounts. Taxes saved later on expenses already reported in the books.
    Permanent difference
    Never reverses, so it creates no deferred tax. It changes the effective tax rate.
    More likely than not
    A likelihood of more than 50%.
