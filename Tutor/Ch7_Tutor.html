Quick formulas

  1. Interest: face × annual rate × months ÷ 12.
  2. Bad debt expense (balance sheet approach): required allowance − pre-adjustment credit balance (or + a debit balance).
  3. Discounted note proceeds: maturity value − (maturity value × bank rate × months left ÷ 12).
  4. Receivables turnover: net sales ÷ average net receivables; days = 365 ÷ turnover.
Cash equivalent
Highly liquid investment maturing within three months of the date it was acquired.
Contra account
Reduces a related account: sales returns and sales discounts reduce revenue; the allowance and discount on notes reduce receivables.
Secured borrowing vs. sale
If the company surrenders control of the receivables, it is a sale; otherwise it is a loan with the receivables as collateral.
