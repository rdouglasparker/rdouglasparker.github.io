Present Value of a Single Amount

Find today's value of one future cash flow.

Example 1

You are promised $15,000 in 5 years. If the discount rate is 8%, what is the present value of that promise today?

$
Calculator Solution
N = 5
I/Y = 8
PMT = 0
FV = 15,000
CPT PV →

PV = $10,208.75 (displays as −$10,208.75 on the calculator, per the cash-flow sign convention)

Present Value of a Deferred Annuity

Equal payments, but the first one doesn't start until more than one period after today. This always takes two calculator steps: value the ordinary annuity as of one period before the first payment, then discount that lump sum back to today.

Example 1

You will receive $6,000 per year for 4 years, but the first payment isn't until 4 years from now (payments occur at the end of years 4, 5, 6, and 7). Your discount rate is 8%. What is the present value today?

$
Calculator Solution (Two Steps)

Step 1: value the 4-payment ordinary annuity as of the end of year 3 (one period before the first payment):

N = 4
I/Y = 8
PMT = 6,000
FV = 0
CPT PV → −$19,872.75 (value as of end of year 3)

Step 2: discount that lump sum back 3 more years to today:

N = 3
I/Y = 8
PMT = 0
FV = 19,872.75
CPT PV →

PV = $15,775.73
