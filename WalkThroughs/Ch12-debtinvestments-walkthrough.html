The bonds used in Problems 2–14

On July 1, 2026, Pioneer Peak Financial purchased $800,000 of 6% bonds issued by Forsyth Fabrication Corp. The bonds are dated July 1, 2026, and pay interest semiannually on June 30 and December 31. They mature on June 30, 2029. On the purchase date, the market rate for bonds of similar risk and maturity is 8%. Pioneer Peak's fiscal year ends December 31.

Transfers Between Classifications

Problem 15 — Reclassifying a Security

A company reclassifies a debt security. The security is transferred at its fair value on the transfer date. How is the unrealized gain or loss at the transfer date handled?

Your answers vs. the answers
Your answers compared with the correct answers for Problem 15
ItemYour answerAnswer
Explanation
  1. HTM → AFS: the security is marked to fair value and the total unrealized gain or loss is reported in AOCI. There's no effect on current income.
  2. AFS → Trading: the total unrealized gain or loss is included in current net income, as if it all occurred now. Trading securities carry all fair value changes through net income.
  3. AFS → HTM: there's no current income effect. The fair value becomes the new amortized cost, and the existing AOCI balance is not written off but is amortized to net income over the security's remaining life.

(Transfers out of trading to HTM or AFS only put the unrealized gain or loss from the current period, before the transfer, into net income. Earlier amounts already went through net income.) Reclassifications are unusual, and the notes must describe the circumstances that led to them.
